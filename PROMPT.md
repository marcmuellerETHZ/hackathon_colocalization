Project description: Most disease-associated variants from GWAS are non-coding and should act by changing something molecular: expression, splicing, chromatin or protein levels. Yet GWAS signals and molecular QTLs rarely colocalize. This happens in both directions: many GWAS hits have no matching molecular QTL, and many molecular QTLs have no link to any trait. Your goal is to optimize colocalization (both speed and power). Benchmark existing colocalization methods and improve on them. The method should cope with overlapping samples and mismatched LD references, and should distinguish two distinct causal variants from a signal that is too weak to call. The project goal and its parts are fixed, the idea is a human prior, not a plan. Keep at least three model families alive through screening, including a bold one. Run the cheapest test that could kill an idea before full exploration. The novelty lies in more speed and better performance. 

Details: Absence of colocalization is not a negative. The meaningful comparison is between a shared variant and two distinct variants that both had enough power to be detected. Conditionally independent signals at one locus can give natural negative controls. Split data by LD region, and keep each gene in a single split. Harmonize all data to the hg38 genome build.
