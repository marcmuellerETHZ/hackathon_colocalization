## 4. Diagnosis (once, A unchanged)

These are descriptive splits of the A1 frame; no threshold or metric was changed. The per-trait LD split is the only post-hoc stratification.

**Per-trait LD did not cause the failure.** A is below coloc-SuSiE on both subsets:

| Subset | A AUPRC | coloc-SuSiE AUPRC |
|---|---|---|
| 848 loci with per-trait LD (249 S / 488 D) | 0.915 | 0.979 |
| 1152 one-LD fallback loci (251 S / 512 D) | 0.872 | 0.980 |

Within each subset, power matching was recomputed with the §8.6 rule.

**Score saturation.** 62.5% of A's loci are in tied-score groups, against 1.9% for coloc-SuSiE. A's s = P_S/(P_S + P_D) is mostly 0 or 1, so A loses ranking resolution. A ranks 2.0% of S loci below 0.5, the same as coloc-SuSiE.

**False S on D loci with moderate LD.** Share of D loci with s > 0.5:

| D r² bin | n | A | coloc-SuSiE |
|---|---|---|---|
| [0, 0.3) | 421 | 1.7% | 2.9% |
| [0.3, 0.5) | 79 | 8.9% | 0.0% |
| [0.5, 0.8) | 500 | 25.8% | 16.6% |

This matches the local-optimum and shared-state attraction the P3-5 multi-start was meant to address. The no-shared-first start was kept on 468/2000 loci.

**One-LD rung on the A1 cell** (`famA_joint_susie_rho_eta_ms`: the same fit without per-trait LD; run `20261027T142758Z_…_5ca348`; 1999 ok, 1 nonconverged):

| Rung | AUPRC | AUROC | median runtime |
|---|---|---|---|
| one-LD | 0.894 | 0.936 | 0.69 s |
| primary | 0.893 | 0.936 | 1.26 s |

- Primary − one-LD = −0.0008, 95% CI [−0.0056, 0.0030] (500 locus-bootstrap replicates).
- So per-trait LD neither helped nor hurt. The subset gap above (0.915 vs 0.872) reflects which loci fall in each subset, not an effect of per-trait LD.

**Reading.** As implemented, the joint model is well behaved: 0 failures, 1 nonconverged, 1999/2000 ok. It is less discriminative than coloc-SuSiE, which fits each trait separately and pairs credible sets, in the ideal setting. At L2/L4 it is also less robust than coloc.abf.
