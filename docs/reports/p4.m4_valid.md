## Inputs and design

- **Locus sample:** m4_subsample@1 (seed derive(20261029, 'm4_subsample'), nested draw in `src/baselines/subsample.py`):
  - **main**, 12,170 loci: 100 S / 100 D / 100 W per cell, plus G and U up to 30 each, over the 34 cells;
  - **slow**, 4,080 loci: 40/40/40 per cell (colocboost, colocboost_m1);
  - **mvsusie**, 2,040 loci: 20/20/20 per cell (P3-17).
- **Timeouts:** the pilot-frozen provisional values (`baselines.run.PILOT_TIMEOUTS_S`).
- **mvSuSiE:** hard lfsr rule primary (P3-2).
- **Evaluation:** `scripts/run_benchmark.py`, driven by `scripts/baselines_p4.py bench` (expected ids = the subsample).
  - Failures are scored least-favourably.
  - 2,000-replicate BCa bootstrap over LD blocks.
  - C3 operating points and recalibration intercepts are fitted on this validation set.
  - strength_only uses a K = 5 cross-fit on the main sample; the slow benchmark reuses the same cross-fitted scores.
- **Artifact files** (`m4_valid_baselines@2`):
  - `main/` and `slow/`: benchmark.json, summary.md, operating_points.json, strength tables, manifest.json;
  - `m4summary.json` / `.md`;
  - `operating_points_final.json` (method → t_S, t_D, recal_a_S, recal_a_D, source);
  - `m4_late_operating_points.json` and `m4_late_coprimaries_coloc_susie.json` (coloc_susie on M4);
  - `screen_fallback_operating_points.json` (P3-27).
- **coloc_susie on M4** was evaluated with `scripts/baselines_p4.py oppoints` and `quickeval`, because a full re-benchmark (about 30 min) did not fit before the freeze. Both use the same metric code as run_benchmark: `oppoints` reproduces the benchmark's coloc_abf W 0.2 t_S, t_D and recalibration values exactly, and `quickeval` runs a joint 2,000-replicate LD-block BCa bootstrap with coloc_abf W 0.2 and strength_only.
  - Source benchmarks: `results/baselines_m4/bench/benchmarks/20261027T182437Z_p4_m4_main_partial` and `…/20261027T185341Z_p4_m4_slow_partial`.

- The full precision values are in `operating_points_final.json`.
- For comparison, the screen fallback for coloc_susie would be t_S 0.8616, t_D 0.8442. Its M4 values are used.
- **About the screen fallback:** screen_v2's "realistic" setting pools only its L2/L4 × P1/P3(ρ = 0.5) cells, so its thresholds come from a different cell mix from M4's.
- **mvsusie under the hard lfsr rule (P3-2):** P_S is binary. More than 2.5% of W loci on screen_v2 have P_S = 1, so t_S falls just above 1 and the method makes no confident S calls at C3. Its P_D is also binary, so t_D is just above 0. This is a property of the pre-registered rule, not tuning.
- Every method's full set is in `main/operating_points.json` and `slow/operating_points.json`.
- The proportional tests and lead_snp have no P_S or P_D, so their thresholds are infinite and they are not C3-eligible.
- **Caveats** (properties of the outputs, not tuning):
  - ColocBoost's P_D is near-binary (D6(f)), so its t_D sits just above 0. Any non-zero P_D counts as a D call.
  - The logit-intercept MLE diverges for near-binary probabilities: sharepro a_S = 446 and colocboost a_S = −1.5e8, under the eval module's 1e-6 clipping. These recalibrated calibration numbers should not be used.
