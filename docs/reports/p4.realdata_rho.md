### Estimator settings

These mirror `sim.generator.rho_hat_for`:
- **Mask:** SNPs with max|z| > 4, plus SNPs with r² > 0.1 to one of them.
- **Pruning:** greedy pruning at r² < 0.1. The visit order is random, seeded by `derive(20261029, "real_rho_prune_order", locus_id)`, so the three R4b LD modes of a pair share one order.
- **Estimate:** truncated MLE on SNPs with |z| < 2, with unit variances.
- **SE:** jackknife over 10 blocks.
- **`residualize_signal=False` everywhere.** The simulator only residualises when the LD is in-sample for both traits or n_ref ≥ 1,000. Neither condition holds here:
  - pool B has n = 150;
  - NFE-minus-GEUVADIS has n = 138;
  - GEUVADIS has n = 445 but is in-sample for trait 2 only.

ρ̂ is now computed **per LD mode**: each file's value uses the LD given to methods in that file. In v1, one value was shared by all three R4b modes.

### Pooled estimate

The pooled estimate is computed from per-window sufficient statistics. A test checks that the point estimate equals `rho_hat_pooled` to within 1e-9.

**Jackknife SE.** The jackknife deletes **clusters of windows that share an LD block** (union-find on `block_ids`). This is needed because neighbouring gene windows overlap, and FinnGen z-scores are identical across genes. The windows-as-blocks SE of `rho_hat_pooled` is also stored, as `rho_se_pair_win`. The two SEs agree closely for large pairs, for example R2 GTEx×Lepik: 0.005 vs 0.004. Validation loci carry their pair's train estimate.

## 3. Does R2 move towards 0? Partly: 0.157 → 0.126 per window, 0.105 pooled. The remainder is shared cis signal, not sample overlap.

The cohorts are donor-disjoint, so true ρ ≈ 0. The signal mask removed about a fifth of the bias, but R2 stays clearly positive. Three diagnostics locate the cause. They are in `rho_v2_diagnostics.json` (train+valid, |z| < 2 SNPs, pooled MLE, jackknife over windows).

1. **Distance profile.** ρ̂ falls with distance to the nearest signal SNP (max|z| > 4).
   - R2 eQTL×eQTL: 0.241 (0–50 kb) → 0.159 (50–100 kb) → 0.116 (100–200 kb) → 0.068 (200–400 kb) → **0.045 ± 0.007 (> 400 kb)**.
   - R4b eQTL×eQTL: 0.277 → 0.192 → 0.140 → 0.090 → 0.057 ± 0.013.
   - FinnGen×eQTL is flat at about 0 in every bin. R3b: −0.004, 0.004, 0.003, 0.011, 0.030 ± 0.012. R4b: −0.005 … −0.006.

   Sample overlap would give a flat profile. A decay with distance is the signature of shared genetic signal that leaks through LD.
2. **Signal strength.** The R2 median ρ̂ rises across quintiles of min(|lead z|): 0.097, 0.112, 0.119, 0.137, 0.157 (median strength 5.7 → 14.2). R4b eQTL×eQTL rises the same way, 0.123 → 0.191. FinnGen×eQTL is flat: R3b −0.003 to 0.010.
3. **Sign of the shared signal.** R2 loci whose strongest joint SNP has *opposite* signs in the two cohorts (n = 99) have median ρ̂ **−0.025**. Concordant loci (n = 10,922) have median 0.129. Sample overlap cannot change sign with the effect direction.

**Interpretation.** In R2, almost every gene has a concordant shared eQTL. Many SNPs with |z| < 2 carry part of that shared signal: they sit in weak LD (r² < 0.1) with a causal variant, or there are sub-threshold secondary shared signals. The 150-person pool-B LD cannot mask these SNPs reliably. The truncated MLE reads the induced z1·z2 correlation as overlap. This is the "concordant-sharing bias" the simulator documents (D14: +0.04–0.08 for P3 eQTL at L2), but larger on real data.

**Consequences.**
- The pooled estimate does not remove the bias: its pooled statistics are unpruned, so it is larger than the per-window estimate in R4b. The P3-1 reversal test ("report both if R2 calls differ between per-window and pooled") therefore **cannot detect it**, because both estimates carry the same bias.
- A method that trusts ρ̂ will attribute part of the true R2 sharing to overlap, which reduces S recall on R2.
- R3b is unaffected (median 0.004), and so are the FinnGen×eQTL pairs of R4b.

## 7. Known limitations

- **Per-window SE is too small at ρ ≈ 0.** In R3b, 10.6% of windows have |ρ̂/SE| > 2, against 5% expected. This is consistent with the simulator's reported SE under-coverage (1.5–1.8×) with small reference panels.
- **NaN per-window ρ̂ increased:** R2 419 (3.7%), R3b 89, R4b pcadj 123. Methods need a fallback rule.
- The attributes were rewritten in place. Any consumer that cached @1 values must re-read.
