## 1. Method

B-mm keeps B's exact enumeration: ≤ 2 causal variants per trait, M = 50 screen, Gaussian EB prior, p1 = p2 = 1e-4, p12 = 5e-6, n_mc = 16,384. It replaces the likelihood with the variance-inflated RSS model of DESIGN layer E.

**Model.**
- z ~ N((I₂⊗R̃)β, Σ), with Σ = V⊗R̃ + Δ̂.
- Δ̂ is the reference-error covariance of (R̂ − R)λ. It is block-diagonal over variants k, with 2×2 trait blocks: Δ_tu,k = (1/n_ref) Σ_jj' E[λ_tj λ_uj' | z] C^(k)_jj'.
- C^(k) is the Olkin–Siotani large-sample covariance of sample correlations that share index k. Its diagonal is (1 − r_kj²)², which is the DESIGN term. Its j ≠ j' entries are the cross terms.
- Cross-trait blocks are included, because the same reference error enters both traits.

**Fitting.**
- λλ' is plugged in as its exact posterior second moment under the current fit, summed over all configurations.
- There are 2 refinements, starting from the phase-1 fit. At each one, (σ_t, η) are re-selected by the phase-1 EB rule under Σ_t(η) = R̃(η) + diag(Δ_tt).

**Likelihood (exact for this Gaussian model).**
- Every configuration's BF is the exact support-space BF of the full-z_M likelihood.
- Φ = X'Σ⁻¹X and b = X'Σ⁻¹z are computed in a Woodbury form that never inverts R̃. At n_ref = ∞ this is exactly phase-1 B.

**Design choices.**
- The plug-in is locus-level. A configuration-dependent Δ would need about 1.6 M dense 100×100 factorisations per locus.
- mm_iter = 2 and cross terms on were fixed before any screen or M4 result. Details are in src/famB/README.md ("B-mm").

## 8. Notes and limitations

- **Where the inflation helps, and where it does not.** The inflation improves ranking: the gate, and L2 and L4 AUPRC at both screen and M4. But it moves B-mm's P_S scale. Its OP1 t_S stays near 0.01–0.02, and at OP2 the D-side constraint binds (t_S 0.51 on the screen, 0.78 on M4). L4 false-S at OP2 is no lower than phase-1 B's: 0.109 for both.
- **What the model omits.** It does not model the L4 allele flips or the missing variants. Only the reference-size variance (and η) is modelled; the DENTIST-S outlier layer is not implemented.
- **Plug-in approximation.** The locus-level plug-in of E[λλ'] approximates a configuration-dependent inflation. The test suite verifies exactness relative to the plug-in model, not relative to the configuration-dependent model.
