## Why the primary and sensitivity readings disagree

The matched-W rule picks the smallest threshold whose confident-S rate on W is ≤ 2.5%. B puts almost no P_S mass on W loci, so its matched-W threshold is **t_S = 0.013**.

The other methods' thresholds are much higher: coloc-SuSiE 0.86, coloc-SuSiE-mm 0.82, coloc.abf 0.78, SharePro 0.66. At P_S ≥ 0.013, B calls S on 22% of Dmid loci.

The same predictions at B's calibrated 0.8 threshold give the reverse picture. This is the sensitivity analysis, not the primary result:

Rule (c) at fixed 0.8 does not kill B:
- At L4, B 0.066 vs colocboost_m1 0.040 and coloc.abf 0.048. Both differences are below +2 pp over 2×.
- At ρ = 0.5, B 0.037 is the lowest of all methods.

Under the primary matched-W reading, B1 per stratum is also clear:
- vs coloc-SuSiE, B is worse in Dmid L0 (−0.172 ± 0.011), Dmid L2 (−0.061 ± 0.017) and Mx L0 (−0.035 ± 0.011);
- B is better only in Mx L2 (+0.024 ± 0.012);
- vs coloc-SuSiE-mm, B is worse in every stratum.

**Caveats on the comparators.**
- mvSuSiE's matched-W threshold exceeds 1 (it never calls S), so its false-S of 0 is degenerate. This is why the second rule-(c) comparator, the best-AUPRC baseline, is also reported. It kills too.
- coloc-SuSiE's L4 claims are blocked by P3-29 (> 1% failures). It is not the rule-(c) comparator.
